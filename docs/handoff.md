Mockups are generated by `tools/mockups.py` (Pillow + numpy, procedural; `python3 tools/mockups.py`);
`tools/mockups_v1.py` is the old version.

Short version of the game: original techno-fantasy city builder in Godot 4 / GDScript, desktop Linux first.
One settlement on a 64x48 square-grid map grows Camp -> Village -> Town -> City. Resources food, materials, Lumen,
gold. Citizens walk, jobs fill automatically. Threat: the seasonal Dimming (Lumen goes quiet, each one longer,
always forecast) plus blight spreading from Lumen pumps. One Spirit meter. Win: City era + survive the Great
Dimming; lose: population 0. No combat in v1; Spore-style neighbours are v2.
