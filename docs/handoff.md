Mockups are generated by `tools/mockups.py` (Pillow + numpy, procedural; `python3 tools/mockups.py`);
`tools/mockups_v1.py` is the old version.

Short version of the game: original techno-fantasy city builder in Godot 4 / GDScript, desktop Linux first,
ONI-like length (6-10 h campaign + endless). One settlement on a 128x96 map of buyable regions grows through seven
eras, Camp to Ascendant. Resources food, materials, Lumen, gold plus refined goods. Citizens walk, jobs fill automatically. Threat: the seasonal Dimming (Lumen goes quiet, each one longer,
always forecast) plus blight spreading from Lumen pumps and a planet health meter that lengthens Dimmings. One Spirit meter. Win (D33): Ascendant era + Skyheart + survive the
Great Dimming with planet health >= 50%; lose: population 0. No combat in v1; Spore-style neighbours are v2.
